About

In-Browser File Encrypter encrypts files with AES-256-GCM, an authenticated cipher, using a key derived from your password with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512. Everything happens locally in this browser tab — your files and key are never sent anywhere, and the page's Content-Security-Policy blocks network requests outright. For extra peace of mind, you can disconnect from the internet once the page has loaded.

This application is provided with no warranty or guarantee of any kind.

Use it at your own risk, and keep a backup of anything important.
